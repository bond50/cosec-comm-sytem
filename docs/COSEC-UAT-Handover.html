1. Purpose

This is the UAT record for the COSEC Communication System: what was tested, what passed, what was fixed, and what still needs a data-rich reporting pass. The system models communication movement and accountability — Receive → Register → Review → Dispatch → Action → Feedback → Close, not generic CRUD.

2. Confirmed Operating Model

  • Office of the County Secretary and Head of Public Service is the central control office.
  • Registry receives and registers correspondence at the central office.
  • Reviewing Officer / Management represents the County Secretary authority path.
  • Line departments (Transport, Health, Finance) are execution units.
  • Users can hold multiple roles, assigned by the administrator.
  • Every active user belongs to a department.
